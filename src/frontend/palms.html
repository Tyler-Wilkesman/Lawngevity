Palms

Commonly grown in tropical climates, U.S. palm trees are found in various warm places. There are options available for both indoor and outdoor growing and in or out of your favorite vessel.

🚚 Delivery Available ✉️ Contact Us

Windmill Palm

A cold hardy palm, which is deer, disease and pest resistant. It is low maintenance, has a growth rate of 6–12 inches per year, and puts a touch of the tropics in your creative space.

Sago Palm

Its unique trunk has great texture, as well as its palm leaves. Adds a warm, tropical feel to your house or yard and is low maintenance.

Pindo Palm

The Pindo's feathered leaf, silvery fronds produce an exceptional contrast to a lawn's color. One of the hardiest palms, doing well in cooler temps, blazing sun, salty air and dry conditions. Pindo palms also generate edible berries that can be made into jelly — the flavor mimics a sweet banana-pineapple flavor.

European Fan Palm

Our slow grower, easy to care for, that can expand 8–15 feet tall and 6–10 feet wide, with tiny yellow blossom clusters in spring. Thanks to its drought resistance and sturdiness in heat, it thrives in severe conditions.
