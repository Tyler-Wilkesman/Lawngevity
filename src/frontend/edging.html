Edging

Edging creates clean lines between beds and various other areas. It can help with lawn maintenance, prevention of weeds and grass spreading into your lawn, keep soil and mulch from eroding, and define pathways made from loose materials.

Permaloc aluminum edging locks in place — 4 inches wide, 16 feet long, available in black and bronze. Our cobblestone block edge is natural grey, hand cut and tumbled for a weathered look.
