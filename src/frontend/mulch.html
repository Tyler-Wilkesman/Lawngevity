Why Mulch?

Mulch Can Provide Many Benefits to Your Plants and Soil

  • Slows weed germination by reducing light availability.
  • Insulates the soil, keeping it cooler in summer and maintaining a uniform temperature through winter.
  • Reduces evaporation, helping to conserve water and reduce watering.
